## Steps

1. Pick 15 mechanics of different kinds (economy, diplomacy, map, battle, dialog, UI, AI, items …)
   from the mod's module_*.py; each with file:line evidence in the mod.
2. For each: one line of what it does, which UW construct would express it (quote the keyword and
   where it is in uw_syntax or a golden test) and the class A/B/C/D. D means "no UW construct";
   say which general mechanism (not which feature) is missing.
3. Write docs/bilgi/stres/Diplado.md (≤ 150 lines): table + totals + the 3 most common missing mechanisms.
