- `cond` (§2, §7.3): `And` -> its parts in sequence; a pure `or` of single conditions that is an element of an `and` chain -> `this_or_next|` on all but the last; any other shape (`(a and b) or c`, a bare top-level `or`) -> a flag temp via `flag_of` (`assign(t,0)`, then `try_begin; <branch>; assign(t,1); try_end` per or-branch, then `eq(t,1)`); `not` on a composite -> the same flag plus `eq(t,0)`.
- `if_stmt` (§2): `try_begin; <cond>; <body>; else_try; <cond2>; <body2>; else_try; <else body>; try_end`.
- Origins: generated structural ops take the originating statement's origin; comparison ops and their expression ops take the `Cond::Cmp` origin; passthrough `Cond::Op` keeps its own.
